Who I Am

Hi! I'm Adrian M. Anunciacion, a BS Information Technology student at Holy Angel University in Angeles City, Philippines. I'm passionate about web development and UI/UX design — I love turning ideas into clean, functional, and visually compelling web experiences.

My journey in tech started with a curiosity about how websites work, and quickly grew into a deep interest in front-end development and user interface design. I focus on writing semantic, accessible, and maintainable code while crafting interfaces that are both beautiful and user-friendly.
